SCIENCE138 • Loudness is a physiological response of the ear to the intensity of sound. • The amount of sound energy passing each second through unit area is called the intensity of sound. • The audible range of hearing for average human beings is in the frequency range of 20 Hz – 20 kHz. • Sound waves with frequencies below the audible range are termed “infrasonic” and those above the audible range are termed “ultrasonic”. • Ultrasound has many medical and industrial applications.
