How does a bullet pier ce the target? How does the wind move the blades of a windmill? Objects in motion possess energy. W e call this ener gy kinetic energy. A falling coconut, a speeding car, a rolling stone, a flying aircraft, flowing water, blowing wind, a running athlete etc. possess kinetic energy. In short, kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its motion. The kinetic energy of an object increases with its speed. How much ener gy is possessed by a moving body by virtue of its motion?
